Three worlds, one forecast

Pick a world and the same forecast is retold in it. The scenes are quiet and a little eerie, never cartoonish, with small movements that keep them alive. Drag through the hours and the painting changes with the weather, from afternoon sun to evening storm to a clear, moonlit night.

  • British Isles

    Moor, standing stones and the old oak. The storm dragon rises with the thunder, the Cailleach brings the frost, will-o’-the-wisps gather in the fog and the Morrígan’s crows come with a falling barometer.

  • Norse

    Fjord, longhouse and the ash tree. Thor’s storms, frost giants in the cold, draugr in the mist, Jörmungandr in the gales, and Valkyries riding the aurora.

  • Chinese Folklore Coming soon

    Lei Gong’s thunder, the Dragon King’s rain, Qingnü’s snow on the summit and Chang’e on the moon.
