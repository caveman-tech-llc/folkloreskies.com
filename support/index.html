Support

Folklore Skies shows the real forecast (from Apple Weather) as a painted folklore landscape, in two worlds, the British Isles and the Norse, with Chinese Folklore coming soon.

Questions and problems

Email support@caveman.cool. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for a forecast problem, the place you were looking at.

Tips

  • Swipe up or down anywhere for expanded or minimal mode; long-press the scene to peek at the details.
  • Drag the hourly strip to move through time; swipe left or right to change day (up to 10 days).
  • In expanded mode, tap the details card for the day’s charts; touch and drag a chart for each hour.
  • The … button (top right) opens Settings: place, recent places, theme, units and motion.
  • Recent places are kept on your iPhone only; swipe one to delete it, or clear the list in Settings.
  • No weather showing? Check your internet connection and, for your current location, that location access is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Folklore Skies.

Themes and purchases

  • Change theme in Settings › Theme. New themes are on the way.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
